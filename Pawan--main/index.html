About Saanjh

Crafted for calm, designed for you

Saanjh is a luxury candle and jewellery brand born from the belief that everyday rituals deserve beauty. Each piece is thoughtfully made to bring warmth, fragrance, and elegance into your home and style.

From hand-poured candles with curated scents to delicate jewellery that complements mindful living, we create products that feel personal, premium, and deeply soothing.

Our aesthetic blends soft neutrals, golden accents, and timeless design — because luxury should feel gentle, not loud.

Help

Shipping & Returns

How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery across India typically takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. You will receive updates on WhatsApp once your order ships.

Free shipping

Enjoy free shipping on all orders above ₹1,000. For orders below that amount, a small delivery fee may apply at checkout — we will confirm the total before dispatch.

Returns & exchanges

Due to the handcrafted nature of our candles and jewellery, we accept returns only for damaged or defective items reported within 48 hours of delivery. Please share photos on WhatsApp and we will make it right.

Candle care

Trim the wick to ¼ inch before each burn. Allow the wax to melt edge-to-edge on the first use. Never leave a burning candle unattended. Keep away from drafts and flammable materials.

Jewellery care

Store pieces in a dry pouch, avoid water and perfume contact, and polish gently with a soft cloth. Remove before sleeping or exercising to maintain the finish.
